# Privacy Policy — Prompt Safe

_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

Prompt Safe collects **no data**.

- All scanning happens locally on your computer.
- The app makes **no network connections**. There is no telemetry, no analytics,
  no crash reporting and no update check.
- Text you paste or open is held in memory only while the app is running. It is
  never written to disk.
- The only thing saved is your UI preferences, such as the selected tab and
  checkbox settings, plus the window size and position. They are stored locally
  in the app's data folder.
- Clipboard access happens only when you click a copy or sanitize button, press
  the sanitize hotkey, or enable the optional 60-second clipboard clearing.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/VolchokLV/prompt-safe/issues
